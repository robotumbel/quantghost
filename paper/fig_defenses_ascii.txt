WHY ALL FOUR LAYERS  (mapped to the trilemma corners)
   The attacker may hold at most 2 of:
     { high-capacity , permutation-invariant , stealthy-quant-robust }.
   - Layer 1 catches the (high-capacity, stealthy-robust) positional attack.
   - Layer 3 removes that positional payload once permutation is applied.
   - Layer 4 catches the (permutation-invariant, stealthy-robust) residual.
   No single layer covers all three corners; only their CONJUNCTION does.
